How it works
  1. 1Join the required channel if the owner has enabled force-subscription.
  2. 2Tap the rewarded-ad button. Do not click ads unless you genuinely want to visit the advertiser.
  3. 3Wait for the ad network’s server-side confirmation, then return to the bot and tap “Get Channel Link”.

Reward verification may take a short time. If no rewarded ad is available, retry later. This app does not promise a reward for an unconfirmed or non-valued event.
